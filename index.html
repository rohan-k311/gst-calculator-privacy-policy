Privacy Policy

App: GST Calculator & Billing (com.vyapar.gstcalculator)
Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: GST Calculator & Billing is an offline application. All business bills, transactions, customer records, and product details remain strictly on your device. We do not operate external databases, track your activity, or transmit your business data to our servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GST Calculator & Billing ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the confidential business records you manage using the App.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The App is designed with an Offline-First architecture:

  • Local Business Data: Products, prices, HSN codes, customer names, contact phone numbers, business profiles, and sales invoices are stored strictly in local on-device database storage (Hive database) within your device's isolated application sandbox.
  • No Cloud Account Required: You do not need to register an account or log in with personal credentials to use the App.
  • No Remote Data Transmission: We do not collect, monitor, upload, sell, or rent your sales records, customer lists, or financial data to any external cloud server or third party.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App requests standard Android device permissions only when necessary to perform offline billing operations:

  • Storage / Media Access: Required to generate, render, save, and share PDF invoices and ledger statements on your phone's storage.
  • Photos / Gallery (Image Picker): Optional. Used only if you choose to select a custom business logo from your gallery to print on your PDF invoices. The selected logo remains stored on your device only.
  • Internet Access: Standard network access used solely when you choose to open user-initiated external actions, such as launching WhatsApp to share an invoice, opening customer phone dials, or viewing external links. The App itself does not transmit analytics or user data over the internet.

4. Third-Party Services & Sharing

The App allows you to share invoices directly with customers via third-party apps like WhatsApp. When you tap "Share on WhatsApp", the generated invoice PDF or summary text is passed directly from your device to the WhatsApp application via standard Android Intents. We do not monitor or access your WhatsApp messages or communications.

The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob) or user analytics trackers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • You can edit or delete individual bills, customers, or products directly within the App at any time.
  • Clearing the App's storage via Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all stored data from your device.
  • You are responsible for backing up your exported PDF files if you switch or reset your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for business merchants, shop owners, and professionals aged 18 and older. It does not knowingly collect or target information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
